📖 About the Movie

Transformers: Age of Extinction is a 2014 American science fiction action film directed by Michael Bay. It is the fourth installment in the live-action Transformers film series.

The story follows Cade Yeager, an inventor who discovers the damaged Autobot leader Optimus Prime. Together, they become involved in a dangerous conflict between humans and Transformers.

📝 Movie Synopsis

After the events of the previous movies, humanity begins hunting down Transformers. Cade Yeager discovers Optimus Prime and helps him escape from those pursuing him.

Meanwhile, a mysterious bounty hunter named Lockdown arrives on Earth, and a corporation called KSI attempts to create its own Transformers.

As the conflict grows, Optimus Prime and the Autobots must fight to protect humanity and save their own kind.
